# The Proximate Composition of Nappi and Its Marketing Methods in Bangladesh

## **Overview**
This repository contains the research data and analytical scripts for the study on **Nappi**, a traditional fermented shrimp product widely consumed by ethnic communities in Bangladesh. The study evaluates the nutritional profile (moisture, protein, ash, fat, and carbohydrates) and investigates the traditional marketing channels in the Moheshkhali and Chaufaldandi areas.

## **Key Research Highlights**
* **Subject:** Traditional fermented shrimp paste (Nappi).
* **Scope:** Comparison of proximate composition across different production sites.
* **Analysis:** Statistical evaluations conducted using **R (version 4.2.3)**.
* **Findings:** Significant variations in nutritional content based on preparation techniques and raw materials.

---

## **Repository Structure**
* **`/Data`**: Contains the proximate analysis values (Moisture, Protein, Ash, Fat, etc.) collected from Moheshkhali and Chaufaldandi.
* **`/Scripts`**: R scripts used for statistical analysis, including standard deviation calculations and comparative visualizations.
* **`/Marketing`**: Documentation or summaries regarding the survey on marketing methods and consumer reach.
